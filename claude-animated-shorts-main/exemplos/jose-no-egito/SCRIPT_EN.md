## Facts and sources

**Biblical text (framed in the script as "the story" / Genesis says):**
- [Genesis 41:1] "When two full years had passed, Pharaoh had a dream." (NIV)
- [Genesis 40:23] The cupbearer "did not remember Joseph; he forgot him."
- [Genesis 41:2-4] Seven sleek, fat cows come up out of the river; seven gaunt cows eat them.
- [Genesis 41:5-7] Second dream: seven good heads of grain swallowed by seven thin heads.
- [Genesis 41:8] Pharaoh troubled; all the magicians and wise men of Egypt; no one could interpret.
- [Genesis 41:9-13] The cupbearer remembers a young Hebrew who interpreted dreams in prison.
- [Genesis 41:14] Joseph "shaved and changed his clothes".
- [Genesis 41:16] "God will give Pharaoh the answer he desires."
- [Genesis 41:29-30, 33-36] Seven years of abundance, then seven of famine; take a fifth of the harvest as a reserve.
- [Genesis 41:40-43] Joseph second only to Pharaoh; signet ring, linen, gold chain, second chariot.
- [Genesis 41:46] Joseph was thirty (visuals only).
- [Genesis 41:47-49] Grain like the sand of the sea; he stopped keeping records, beyond measure.
- [Genesis 41:53-57] Famine in all lands; Joseph opens the storehouses; all the world came to Egypt to buy grain.
- [Genesis 47:24-26] A fifth to Pharaoh becomes a lasting statute "to this day".

**History (independent of the Joseph narrative; never claimed as Joseph's silos):**
- Tell Edfu silos: seven round mud-brick silos (5.5-6.5 m) by an administrative building, 17th Dynasty (c. 1630-1520 BC), dig by Nadine Moeller (Univ. of Chicago); grain worked as currency, the silos as "a kind of bank". Source: Current World Archaeology, "Early Egyptian silos".
- Model of a Granary with Scribes, tomb of Meketre, Thebes, c. 1981-1975 BC: scribes record grain. Source: The Met, object 545281.
- Nile flood and famine; nilometers used to forecast harvest and set taxes. Sources: Wikipedia "Nilometer"; National Geographic 2016 (Thmuis).
- Domed mud-brick silos filled from the top, Old Kingdom onward. Source: Sidestone Press, "Storage in Ancient Egypt and Nubia" (2023).
- NOT used: the Famine Stela (Ptolemaic, ~2,000 years after the events, likely propaganda).

## Narration (voice file text)

Broke a week before payday? Ancient Egypt solved that with a nightmare about cows.
It's ancient Egypt, the river floods every summer, and nobody worries about next year.
One night Pharaoh dreams seven fat cows climb out of the Nile, and seven skinny cows eat them.
He dozes off, and dreams it again, with grain.
Every magician in Egypt is summoned, and not one can explain it.
Then the cupbearer remembers a prisoner who's good with dreams, whom he forgot to mention for two full years.
Joseph gets a shave and tells Pharaoh the answer comes from God: seven fat years, then seven starving years. Lock away a fifth of every harvest while it lasts.
Pharaoh makes him second in command on the spot, and the grain piles up until the scribes give up counting.
Then the river drops, the fields crack, and famine hits every land in sight.
Luckily, Egypt had seven years of grain in storage.
Unluckily, the entire world now knew where it was.
You see, ever get a raise and still have nothing left at the end of the month? That's seven fat cows, eaten.
Joseph skimmed a slice off the top before anyone got used to it, back when there was something to skim.
Real Egyptian towns had giant mud-brick silos right beside the scribes' office: grain was money, and the silo was the bank.
So next time someone tells you to pay yourself first, remember: it worked for a man fresh out of jail with a brand new shave.

## Open questions and risks
- Beat 9 "the river drops" is the historical famine mechanism, not Genesis text. Fine as a visual.
- Beat 15 "scribes' office" paraphrases "administrative building"; "the silo was the bank" paraphrases "a kind of bank".
- Seven silos at Tell Edfu is a coincidence with the biblical seven; consider showing six so it is not read as "proof".
- Beat 16 ends on the fifth becoming a permanent levy (Genesis 47:24-26). Accurate, but cynical-sounding; alternative ending above.
- Beat 13 analogy stays inside the Egyptian 3D world, not a modern office.
- Respect: God named once, in Joseph's own framing (41:16). Jokes land on cows, magicians, cupbearer, scribe, queue.
- TTS: test "whom he forgot to mention"; swap to "who forgot to mention him" if it stumbles.
- Never claim: Famine Stela as evidence, a date for Joseph, "first emergency fund in history".
